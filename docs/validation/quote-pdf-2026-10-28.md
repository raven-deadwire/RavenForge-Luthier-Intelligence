The drawing-derived checks agree within 0.5%. They validate internal geometry,
not a manufactured instrument. EDDA's `.712` registration is a visual body
registration, not an independent rear measurement. Its head uses `.62` instead;
therefore the front head at the same stated print scale is about **12.92% smaller**
than its registered rear counterpart (`.62/.712`). There is no single verified
physical calibration for both complete EDDA views. The patch labels EDDA scales
as approximate and adds a compact dimensional-use note, without inventing new
geometry or changing the approved visual design.

Physical printer scaling, real instrument length/width, paper output measured
with a ruler, and production/CNC geometry remain **NOT VERIFIED** for all models.
Required manual check: print A4 at 100% (disable fit-to-page), measure the printed
scale/fret references, and compare with approved dimensioned orthographic
source drawings or a measured instrument. EDDA requires those references before
its full silhouette can be called dimensionally accurate.

## Regression additions

`node tools/test_configurator.cjs --pdf-layout` downloads 48 PDFs: four models ×
three languages × empty/prose/multiline/hyphenated instruction profiles. Long
profiles exercise the 1,000-character boundary. It checks retained text and total,
both images, language restoration, image CSS-to-mm dimensions, page bounds,
1–2-page layouts and cross-language parity. The independent PDF inspection now
also rejects more than two pages. A separate fret-spacing test avoids merely
comparing two copies of the same conversion constant.

The initial all-pixel whitespace scan is already fixed in #65 and is not reported
as an outstanding defect. Contract tests retain the verawood/S-Lock requirements
and all 577 allowed option substitutions × 3 languages × front/back checks pass.
