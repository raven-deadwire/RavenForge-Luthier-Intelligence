|---|---:|---:|---|

| ASKR | 0.946 | 0.946 | First-to-12th fret endpoints imply 937.598 / 861.560 mm vs 939.8 / 863.6 mm nominal |
| GRAMR | 1.0675 | 1.0675 | First-to-12th fret span implies 647.780 mm vs 647.7 mm nominal |

The drawing-derived checks agree within 0.5%. They validate internal geometry,
not a manufactured instrument. EDDA's `.712` registration is a visual body
registration, not an independent rear measurement. Its head uses `.62` instead;
therefore the front head at the same stated print scale is about **12.92% smaller**
than its registered rear counterpart (`.62/.712`). There is no single verified
physical calibration for both complete EDDA views. The patch labels EDDA scales
as approximate and adds a compact dimensional-use note, without inventing new
geometry or changing the approved visual design.

Physical printer scaling, real instrument length/width, paper output measured
with a ruler, and production/CNC geometry remain **NOT VERIFIED** for all models.
Required manual check: print A4 at 100% (disable fit-to-page), measure the printed
scale/fret references, and compare with approved dimensioned orthographic
source drawings or a measured instrument. EDDA requires those references before
its full silhouette can be called dimensionally accurate.

also rejects more than two pages. A separate fret-spacing test avoids merely
comparing two copies of the same conversion constant.

The new baseline browser run [40611297598](https://github.com/raven-deadwire/RavenForge-Luthier-Intelligence/actions/runs/40611297598)
(candidate artifact `12782963198`, commit `e27b855`) produced 48 PDFs / 69 pages.

about 6357 px. Page-count/model/total OCR still passed, demonstrating why those
checks alone did not catch this defect. The fix permits oversized hyphenated
tokens to wrap while keeping shorter tokens together.

Nine additional initial prose-bound violations were test false positives:
`white-space: pre-wrap` legally hangs invisible trailing spaces beyond the line.
The observer now measures non-whitespace token ranges; it does not increase the
bounds tolerance or ignore visible overflow. A separate OCR false negative on
the GRAMR two-column layout was confirmed by inspecting its rendered page. The
visual test retries automatic page segmentation (PSM 3) when uniform-block PSM 6
misses the exact model/total. No expected value is substituted for OCR output.
The 24 first-fix PDFs passed the corrected independent visual check locally.
